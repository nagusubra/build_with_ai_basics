A Flutter dating app for people who find dating apps overwhelming and small talk hard. Profiles are built around how you cook and eat, and after a match, Cherry turns an awkward first chat into a confirmed virtual cooking date, then leaves the two of you to it.

Cherry's rules

Cherry is a natural mutual friend, not a chatbot. These rules decide the demo's "oh, that's cool" moment.

Speaks when…the conversation stalls, someone struggles to reply, or there's a natural opening.
Stays quiet when…both people are actively talking and getting to know each other.
Suggests the date when…both are comfortable and interested, or either one asks.
Never…forces the conversation or the date, or pretends a date is arranged.
Backs off when…someone isn't ready. One gentle reminder if someone goes quiet, then it pauses.
Leaves when…dish, day, and time are confirmed by both and the recipe and song are shared, or when either person asks.

Look and Feel

Warm, playful, modern, and premium, never childish or overwhelming. "A cosy little space where meeting someone new is exciting rather than intimidating." Rounded cards, soft shadows, subtle animations, expressive emojis, plenty of breathing space. The two 3D cherries are the identity.

Decisions, boundaries, and open questions

❓ Open questions
  • What "stuck" means: how long a silence before Cherry steps in. tune in build
  • How both confirm: tap a button or reply in chat. spec
✅ Product decisions
  • Food is the personality, instead of a written bio.
  • Non-cooks are welcome: "can't cook but would love to learn."
  • Quiz, not form: fun, 3–5 minutes, essentials only.
  • Profile data serves Cherry first; no personality algorithm.
  • Real email accounts with login: the team wants "a full functioning app" (Firebase preferred, for spec).
  • Real picture verification, from scope.
  • Cherry is a mutual friend, not a chatbot.
  • A date is only real when both confirm.
  • Music is a suggestion, not playback.
  • Friends or a partner, nothing casual. Each only sees their own kind; Cherry always plans a cooking date.
  • No dead end in Discover: recipes to cook instead.
  • Verification can be retried, not skipped.
  • Cherry's look: by gender; random for same-gender matches and other genders.
  • Reminder is in-chat, not a push notification.
  • Account basics: preview, edit, delete. Delete means everything is deleted, for data privacy.
⏸️ Deferred from the POC
  • Food filters and normal dating filters in Discover (may return later).
  • Recommendation or matching algorithm.
  • Push notifications (reminder is in-chat).
  • Log out, unmatch, block or report, settings. Block/report matters for a real launch's safety.
  • The virtual cooking date itself, e.g. video call.
  • Music playback or Spotify.
🚫 Non-goals
  • Cherry replying to every message.
  • Pressuring anyone into a date.
  • Showing or keeping the verification selfie.
  • Long bios.
