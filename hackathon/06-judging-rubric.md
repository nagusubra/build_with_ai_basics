# Judging rubric

Sources: Official Rules section 6 (https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/rules) and the criteria listed on https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/.

If the website and the Official Rules differ, the Official Rules prevail.

## Stage one: pass or fail

A submission moves on only if it reasonably fits the theme and was built with the Devpost Learn skill pack. The evidence is the planning documents in the repository (`devpost/scope.md`, `devpost/prd.md`, and `devpost/spec.md`).

## Stage two: four equally weighted criteria

Every submission that passes stage one is scored on these, at the judges' discretion. Weights are equal.

1. **Design.** Does the project deliver a complete, coherent product experience, beyond a technical proof of concept?
2. **Potential Impact.** Does the project make a credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience, and does the solution actually address that problem based on what is demonstrated?
3. **Innovation/Idea.** How creative and novel is the concept, and does the project differ from existing concepts?
4. **Presentation.** Does the video clearly demonstrate the project working end to end? Does the pitch communicate what problem is solved, who it is for, and why it matters? Is the overall presentation easy to follow?

The hackathon overview page lists three criteria and omits Potential Impact: Design, Innovation/Idea, and Presentation. Build and present with all **four** official criteria in mind.

## Tie-break

The tied submission with the higher score on the first criterion (Design) ranks higher. If still tied, compare Potential Impact, then Innovation/Idea, then Presentation. If still tied on every criterion, the judges vote.

## How judging may run

Devpost may use expert panels, peer review, automated analysis, or a combination. Judges may be employees or outside reviewers, may change during judging, and may or may not match the names on the site. There may be more than one round.

Judges are not required to clone or run the project. The video and the written description carry the demonstration. Judging runs October 27, 2026, 10:00 am ET through October 30, 2026, 5:00 pm ET.
