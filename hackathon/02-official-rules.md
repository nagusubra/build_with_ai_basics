# Official rules

Source: https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/rules

This is a working summary of the operative rules. If a hackathon page, email, or these notes ever disagree with the Official Rules, **the Official Rules control**. Devpost Terms of Service (https://info.devpost.com/terms) also apply. If those terms conflict with the Official Rules, the Official Rules control for this hackathon. Privacy: https://info.devpost.com/privacy.

No purchase is required to enter or win. Submitting an entry is agreement to the Official Rules as a contract between you (and each teammate), the sponsor, and Devpost.

## How to enter

1. Join at https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/ with a free Devpost account.
2. Install the skill pack. This repo already has it. Command, if you ever need it again: `npx skills add challengepost/learn-ai-basics --all -y`. You need Git, Node.js, and an agent that reads `SKILL.md`.
3. Build a project that meets [04-project-requirements.md](04-project-requirements.md).
4. Complete every required field on the Enter a Submission page during the submission period. See [05-submission-checklist.md](05-submission-checklist.md).

Entry is consent for the sponsor and Devpost to collect and keep personal information to run and publicize the hackathon.

## Teams and organizations

An individual may join more than one team or organization and may also enter alone. A team or organization appoints one Representative who is eligible and who submits on its behalf.

You may submit more than one project. Each submission must be unique and substantially different from your others, as Devpost decides.

## Ownership and intellectual property

The submission must be your original work (or your team's or organization's), solely owned by you, and free of other people's IP claims. You may hire technical help if the ideas, creativity, and rights stay yours. Open source is allowed if you comply with those licenses and your work builds on them.

You keep ownership of the submission. By entering, you give the sponsor a non-exclusive license to use the entry for judging. The sponsor and Devpost may promote the submission and use contributors' name, likeness, voice, and image in materials about the hackathon during the event and for three years after. You also consent to publicity use of name, photo, voice, opinions, comments, and hometown and country, worldwide, unless the law forbids it.

The project must not have been developed with financial or preferential support from the sponsor or administrator (funding, a contract, or a commercial license from them before the submission period ends).

## Testing access

Provide a public repository link and a demo video. If a site is private, put login credentials in the testing instructions. The project must stay free and unrestricted for the sponsor, administrator, and judges through the end of judging. Judges may score from the text, images, and video alone. They are not required to run the code.

Unusual hardware (beyond phones, tablets, and desktop computers) may need to be provided physically if requested.

## Language

Submission materials must be in English, or you must provide an English translation of the video, description, testing instructions, and other materials.

## Prizes, liability, disputes

Prize amounts, verification, taxes, and the one-prize rule are in [07-prizes.md](07-prizes.md). Judging is in [06-judging-rubric.md](06-judging-rubric.md).

Released parties (organizers, prize suppliers, and related people) are released from claims arising from entry, participation, and prize use, within the limits in the Official Rules. Disputes go to individual binding arbitration under the American Arbitration Association, governed by the Federal Arbitration Act, with New York substantive law for interpreting the rules. Class actions and punitive damages are waived where the law allows.

Devpost can cancel, suspend, or change the hackathon for technical failure, fraud, or events outside their control, and can disqualify people who tamper, break the rules, or create a conflict. Rules can be amended by posting on the hackathon site. Questions: jholmes@devpost.com.
