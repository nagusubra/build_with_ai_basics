# Eligibility

Source: Official Rules, section 3, https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/rules

The hackathon site also says ages 18+ only, all countries and territories except the standard exceptions below.

## Who can enter

- An individual who is at least the age of majority where they live at the time of entry.
- A team of those individuals.
- An organization (company, nonprofit, LLC, partnership, or other legal entity) that already exists when it enters.

The Devpost submission form includes an **age of majority checkbox**. It is required for prize eligibility. See [11-organizer-updates.md](11-organizer-updates.md).

## Who cannot enter

- Residents of, or organizations domiciled in, a place where U.S. or local law bars participating or receiving a prize. The rules name **Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, and North Korea**, plus any other country comprehensively sanctioned by the U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control.
- Organizations involved in designing, producing, promoting, running, or distributing the hackathon, including Devpost.
- Employees, representatives, and agents of those organizations, and their immediate family or household.
- Anyone else involved in designing, producing, promoting, running, or distributing the hackathon, and their immediate family or household.
- Any judge, or a company or person that employs a judge.
- A parent, subsidiary, or other affiliate of any organization above.
- Anyone whose participation would create a real or apparent conflict of interest, in the sponsor's or administrator's discretion.

Immediate family means spouse, children and stepchildren, parents and stepparents, and siblings and stepsiblings. Household means anyone who shares the residence for at least three months of the year.

Agents means people or organizations creating a submission for a promotion entity, at that entity's direction, under a contract or similar relationship.

## Teams

If you enter as a team or organization, one eligible person (the Representative) registers and submits for the group. That person warrants they are authorized to act for the team. An eligible person may be on more than one team and may also submit as an individual. Each submission still has to be substantially different.
