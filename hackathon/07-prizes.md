The overview's shorter line for first place ("$1,250 plus a feature in the Devpost newsletter and on Devpost's socials") matches the cash. The Official Rules give the newsletter feature, interview or profile, and social posts to **all three** cash places.

## Participation prizes

Every eligible submission receives:

- A Devpost Learn badge on the Devpost profile.
- A special role in the Devpost Discord.

Each project may receive **one cash prize and one participation prize**. A project is not eligible for two cash prizes.

## Getting paid

- A prize goes to the individual, to the team's Representative, or to the organization. The Representative decides how a team splits it.
- You are not a winner until identity, qualifications, and your role in the submission are verified and any winner affidavit is complete. An announcement alone is not final.
- Required forms are due **10 business days** after they are sent. Wrong details can delay delivery, disqualify you, or forfeit the prize.
- Delivery is within **60 days** after Devpost receives the completed forms. Payment can be mailed or sent to a bank account.
- You pay fees (wire, currency exchange) and all taxes. U.S. residents may need Form W-9. Others may need Form W-8BEN. Devpost may withhold for tax rules.
- Prizes are not transferable. Devpost may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. If nobody is eligible, a prize may go unawarded.

Sponsor, in its discretion, will not award a prize that would create a real or apparent conflict of interest, including projects developed with financial or preferential support from the sponsor or administrator.
